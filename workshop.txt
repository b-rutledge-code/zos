description=[h2]How It Works[/h2]

description=
description=Find [b]ZORK I[/b], [b]ZORK II[/b], or [b]ZORK III[/b] floppies in electronics stores, bookstores, desks, bedrooms, or university. One disk in A: at a time.
description=
